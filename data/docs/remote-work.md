# Nimbus Robotics: Remote Work Guidelines

Nimbus operates on a hybrid model. Employees in the Austin office are expected on site on Tuesdays and Thursdays; all other days are flexible.

Fully remote arrangements must be approved by a VP and are reviewed every 6 months. Remote employees are expected to overlap with the 10:00-15:00 Central Time core hours.

## Home office stipend

New hires receive a one-time $750 home office stipend, claimable within 90 days of their start date. After that, every employee gets a $50 monthly internet reimbursement.

Equipment purchased with the stipend belongs to the employee. Company laptops remain Nimbus property and must be returned within 14 days of leaving the company.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 30 days per year with manager approval. Longer stays require sign-off from the People team for tax and legal reasons.
