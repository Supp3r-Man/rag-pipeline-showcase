# Nimbus Robotics: Expense Policy

Submit expenses in the Harbor portal within 30 days of purchase. Receipts are required for any expense over $25.

## Travel

Book flights at least 14 days in advance when possible. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Hotel stays are capped at $220 per night in major cities and $160 elsewhere.

Meals while traveling are reimbursed up to $75 per day.

## Approvals

Expenses under $500 are approved by your manager. Expenses between $500 and $5,000 require director approval. Anything above $5,000 requires VP approval and a purchase order created by the Finance team.

## Software purchases

Do not buy software with a personal card. Request licenses through the IT team, which maintains an approved vendor list. New vendors go through a security review that takes about 10 business days.
