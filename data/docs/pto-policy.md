# Nimbus Robotics: Time Off Policy

Full-time employees at Nimbus Robotics receive 24 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year. PTO accrues monthly at 2 days per month, starting on the first day of employment.

Unused PTO carries over into the next year up to a maximum of 8 days. Anything above the cap is forfeited on January 31st.

Requests of 5 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 3 weeks in advance through the Harbor portal and approved by your manager.

Nimbus also observes a company-wide shutdown between December 26th and December 31st. Shutdown days do not count against your PTO balance.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 10 sick days per year, which do not carry over. A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.
